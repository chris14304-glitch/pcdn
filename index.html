What We Do

Straightforward cybersecurity information

We provide clear, practical information about online threats and how to stay safe — written for real people, not just technical experts.

Threat Awareness

Clear explanations of current threats including phishing, vishing, social engineering, scams, and how to recognize them before they cause harm.

Safe Practices

Practical guides on safe browsing, strong passwords, two-factor authentication, privacy settings, and securing personal devices.

Educational Resources

In-depth guides covering data breaches, exploit awareness, digital privacy, mobile security, web security, and more — in plain language.

Timely Alerts

Up-to-date notifications about known vulnerabilities, active scam campaigns, data breaches, and emerging threats affecting everyday users.

Important Disclaimer

The Public Cyber Defense Network is an independent informational organization. We do not sell services, accept donations, or operate for profit. We are not a nonprofit, and we are not affiliated with any government, company, or institution. We do not perform hacking activities, penetration testing, or offensive security operations. We do not collect personal data or offer security services. Our sole purpose is to make cybersecurity knowledge more transparent, understandable, and accessible to the public.
