Frequently Asked Questions
How do I update my payment method?▼
Navigate to Payments in the sidebar, then click "Update Payment Method." You can add a new credit/debit card or bank account for automatic payments.
How do I add a new vehicle to my auto policy?▼
You can submit a service request from this page by selecting "Add/Remove Vehicle" above, or call your agent directly. Changes typically take effect within 24 hours.
Where can I find my auto ID card?▼
Go to Documents in the sidebar. Your current auto ID card is always available there for download or you can save it to your phone's wallet.
How do I file a new claim?▼
Visit the Claims Status page and click "File a New Claim," or contact your carrier directly. For emergencies, always call 911 first, then contact your carrier's 24/7 claims hotline listed on your policy documents.
When is my next payment due?▼
Your next payment date and amount are shown on the Dashboard overview and the Payments page. If you have auto-pay enabled, the payment will be processed automatically on the due date.
How do I cancel a policy?▼
Submit a service request selecting "Policy Change" or contact your agent. Please note that cancelling coverage may leave you unprotected — we recommend having replacement coverage in place before cancelling.
